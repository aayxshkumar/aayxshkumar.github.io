Benchmarking · Oct 2026

My p50 was exactly 42 ns. That was the clock, not the code.

Apple Silicon's steady_clock ticks at 24 MHz, so every sample is a multiple of about 41.7 ns, and anything faster shows up as a single tick. Now the latency tool measures the clock's resolution first and warns when it's too coarse to trust. I use throughput runs for averages and Linux for percentiles.

Testing · Oct 2026

My first differential test passed with a planted bug in it.

The random flow crossed the spread so often that queues never formed, so priority bugs had nowhere to show up. Once I biased the flow towards passive orders and added explicit "grow at the same price" modifies, it caught 5 out of 5 planted bugs. Lesson: test your tests.
